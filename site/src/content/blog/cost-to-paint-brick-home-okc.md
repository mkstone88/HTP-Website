The short answer: painting the brick on a full home exterior in the OKC metro usually runs **$6,000 to $12,000**. Here's what moves the number inside that range, what can push it beyond, and why homeowners keep choosing it over the alternatives.

![Large white painted-brick farmhouse-style home in the OKC metro](/uploads/2026/01/painted-brick-white-farmhouse-okc.webp)

## What's included in that range

A professional brick paint job isn't just spraying paint at a wall. For that $6,000–$12,000 you should expect:

- **A full power wash** of the exterior, so the mineral paint bonds to clean masonry.
- **Crack and repair prep.** Small cracks get filled, and here's a nice bonus: paint permanently hides brick repairs and mismatched mortar.
- **Brick-specific mineral products.** We use breathable Romabio products made for masonry — ordinary house paint traps moisture inside brick and damages it over time. Romabio backs its Masonry Flat with a 20-year product warranty when properly applied.
- **A sample wall.** Because painted brick is permanent, we paint and distress one wall first and you approve it before the rest of the house is touched.
- **Proper application**: sprayed from multiple directions to cover every face of every brick, then back-brushed into the texture.

Most projects take about three days, and even big ones finish inside a week.

## What moves the price

**Size is the biggest factor** — more square feet of brick means more product and more time, so a compact single-story lands near the bottom of the range and a large two-story near the top.

**The woodwork is the other big one.** "$6,000–$12,000" covers the brick itself. If we're also painting the trim, fascia, soffits, shutters, and doors — and most homeowners do the whole exterior at once, since the new brick color usually makes old trim look dated — that adds to the total. The upside: one crew, one mobilization, one cohesive result, like the off-white brick and black woodwork combination the Newton family chose in Edmond ([their story and video are here](/brick-painting-okc/)).

**The finish choice matters less than you'd think.** Solid coverage and [limewash-style distressed finishes](/painted-brick-vs-limewash-okc/) are in the same general cost neighborhood — choose the look you love, not the price tag.

## How it compares to the alternatives

The reason brick painting keeps growing in OKC: it's the most complete exterior transformation you can give a home **without a remodel**. Replacing siding, or re-cladding a home in new brick or stone, is a far bigger project — painting the brick typically gets you a completely new look for **about a third of the price** of that kind of exterior remodel, in days instead of weeks.

Against a standard exterior repaint, brick painting sits a bit higher: a typical (non-brick) exterior repaint in the metro runs [$4,000–$10,000](/exterior-painting-oklahoma-city/), while brick work adds masonry-specific products and more involved application.

## One honest caveat before you spend anything

Painting brick is effectively **permanent** — removing paint from brick afterward is technically possible but very difficult. That's not a reason to avoid it; it's a reason to do it once, correctly, with the right products and a sample wall you've approved.
